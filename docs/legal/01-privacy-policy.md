# 📄 RideNow — Privacy Policy

> **Effective Date**: August 8, 2026  
> **Last Updated**: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **RideNow Platform Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** ("RideNow", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal data when you use our mobile applications (Rider App and Driver App), website, and services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information Provided by Users
- **Account Data**: Name, phone number, email address, profile picture.
- **Rider Data**: Saved pickup/drop locations, payment preference.
- **Driver Data**: Driving licence, vehicle registration (RC), vehicle insurance, vehicle inspection details, government photo identification (Aadhaar/PAN details for verification & tax compliance).

### B. Information Automatically Collected
- **Location Information**: 
  - **Riders**: Precise location collected when application is in use to determine pickup points and track ride progress.
  - **Drivers**: Precise background location collected when Driver App is in **ONLINE** status (even when the app is minimized or phone screen is locked) to match nearby rides, provide turn-by-turn navigation, and calculate fares.
- **Transaction Information**: Ride details, date, time, distance, route, payment amount, payment status.
- **Device & Log Information**: IP address, device model, operating system version, device identifier, FCM push tokens.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

- **Service Delivery**: To connect riders with drivers, calculate fares, process payments, and facilitate communication.
- **Safety & Security**: To verify driver credentials, track live trips for safety, implement Emergency SOS features, and detect fraudulent activity or fake GPS locations.
- **Customer Support**: To investigate ride issues, fare disputes, lost items, and payment queries.
- **Legal & Regulatory Compliance**: To comply with taxation obligations (GST, TDS deduction), motor vehicle rules, and government mandates under applicable laws (including Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Privacy Controls

- **Rider-Driver Sharing**: Riders see driver's first name, photo, vehicle category, vehicle registration number, and live location during an active trip. Drivers see rider's pickup and drop addresses and first name during an active trip.
- **Phone Number Privacy**: Phone calls between riders and drivers are routed through virtual masked numbers whenever available to prevent disclosure of personal phone numbers.
- **Third-Party Service Providers**: We share data only with verified providers essential for platform operations:
  - Payment Aggregators (e.g., Razorpay) for transaction processing.
  - Cloud Infrastructure (AWS / GCP / Cloudflare) for hosting and database storage.
  - Push Notification Providers (Google FCM / Apple APNs).
  - Mapping Services (Google Maps API).

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

- All data in transit is encrypted using Industry Standard TLS 1.3 encryption.
- Financial records and government identifiers are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
- We do **NOT** store credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs on our servers.

---

## 5. Data Retention & User Rights

- **Retention**: Account and transaction records are retained for 7 years to satisfy statutory tax and legal requirements. Real-time GPS location breadcrumbs are automatically purged after 90 days.
- **Account Deletion**: You can request account deletion via App Settings. Personal identifiers are soft-deleted immediately and permanently erased/anonymized within 30 days, subject to legally required retention exceptions.
