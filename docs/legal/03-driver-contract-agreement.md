# 🤝 RideNow — Driver Partner Agreement

> **Independent Contractor Agreement for Driver Partners**

This Agreement is entered into between **RideNow Platform Technologies Pvt. Ltd.** ("Company") and the undersigned Driver Partner ("Driver").

---

## 1. Independent Contractor Relationship

- Driver operates as an **independent business entity / contractor**. Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating an employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship.
- Driver has complete discretion over when, where, and for how long they log into the Driver App and accept ride requests.

---

## 2. Driver Eligibility & Vehicle Requirements

- Driver must hold a valid Commercial Driving Licence, appropriate badge (if required by local transport authorities), and valid government photo identification.
- The vehicle must possess a valid Commercial Registration Certificate (RC), Commercial Insurance Policy, Fitness Certificate, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate.
- Driver must submit clear copies of all required documents to the Company and maintain active approvals.

---

## 3. Platform Commission & Earnings Settlement

- **Commission Structure**: The Company shall deduct a platform commission percentage (as configured in the system and displayed in the Driver App) from the gross fare of every completed ride.
- **Online Payments**: Fares collected via digital payments shall be credited to Driver's Platform Wallet after deducting platform commission.
- **Cash Payments**: For cash rides, Driver collects the full fare directly from the rider. The Company's commission amount will be debited from Driver's Wallet balance. Driver agrees to maintain a positive wallet balance to continue receiving ride requests.
- **Payouts**: Driver can request payouts of settled earnings to their verified bank account subject to minimum threshold rules.
- **Tax Deductions (TDS)**: Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194O / applicable tax regulations will be withheld by the Company where statutory thresholds apply.

---

## 4. Vehicle Standards & Safety Compliance

- Driver agrees to operate the vehicle safely and strictly adhere to traffic regulations.
- Driver agrees never to allow unauthorized third parties to drive the registered vehicle while logged into the App.
- Driver shall maintain high standards of vehicle cleanliness and professional courtesy.

---

## 5. Termination & Suspension

- Either party may terminate this Agreement at any time without cause.
- The Company reserves the right to immediately suspend or deactivate Driver's account for:
  - Document expiration or invalidity.
  - Using fake GPS applications or fraudulent manipulation of ride data.
  - Serious safety complaints or violations of code of conduct.
  - Driver rating falling below the minimum platform threshold.
